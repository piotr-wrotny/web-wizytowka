# Prototype Instructions

Visual direction, typography, colour, layout and motion are deliberately not prescribed here. Only content and business rules are binding.

## Content and business rules

- 2026-11-22, founder: production is deployed only from `deploy/cloudflare`. Branches move fast-forward only: `dev` → `main` → `deploy/cloudflare`; the deploy branch always equals what is live. Never deploy without the founder's go-ahead.
- 2026-11-22, founder: the visual standard (colour, type, elements, layout, motion) is documented in `docs/standard-wizualny.md`. It describes the approved `redesign/reimagined` direction as built; keep it in sync with `src/styles.css` when the look changes.
- 2026-11-22, founder: the substantive knowledge (offer, methodology, glossary, analyses, situations, client work) lives in `~/Work/ai-ekspert`, which is the source of truth. `src/content` holds published copies; take new or changed content from there, never from client folders.
- Founder name is **Piotr Wrotny** (confirmed 2026-11-09). Public biography, contact, domain and real client evidence remain placeholders. No invented clients, metrics, testimonials or certifications. No public prices in this prototype.
- 2026-11-09, founder: the primary service is **bespoke AI consulting** ("konsultacje AI szyte na miarę"). Standard dla zespołu (W), Jedna decyzja (K) and Przegląd oferty (R, enquiry-only) are the three delivery formats under that umbrella, with W the most elaborate. Do not turn expertise domains into additional services.
- 2026-11-10, founder: the **main use case and main example of work** on the site is the consultation itself. Each stage is shown with its value for the client. Content lives in `site.flow`; the sheet contents are illustrative and the wording is `DRAFT` until approved.
- 2026-11-22, founder: the stages of `site.flow` follow the client path (`~/Work/ai-ekspert/oferta/sciezka-klienta.md`, stages 1–5): **Rozmowa** (short, free), **Rozpoznanie** (questions by e-mail, Karta rozpoznania and meeting programme; accepting the programme is accepting the scope; the date is booked here), **Konsultacja w firmie** (presentation, 2–3 of the client's use cases on demonstration data, group discussion), **Kompendium** (the paid product: general modules plus client chapters), **Dalej** (optional). This replaces the 2026-11-10 cut (recognition, material, presentation, meeting, documents). There is one process on the site. Since 2026-11-23 it is presented in full only on the service page `/konsultacja/`; the home page names it once, as four steps on one screen (`site.story.steps`), and links there. Do not show the path a third time.
- 2026-11-22, founder: positioning is an independent AI expert for **consultation, not implementation**. The product is documents, not hours. Implementation may appear only as a conclusion of a meeting, never as an offer.
- 2026-11-22, founder: stage 0, **open knowledge**, is a free guide (PDF for an e-mail address, global, dated, with a promise of updates) on `/przewodnik/`, with an optional stack selection and a synthetic sample compendium on `/materialy/przykladowe-kompendium/` that stays readable without leaving contact. It is stage 0 of the path on the service page; its sheet links to the guide and the analyses. Since 2026-11-23 the home page offers the same reading list under the contact card ("Jeszcze nie teraz?") for the visitor who is not ready to talk. Content lives in `site.guide` and `site.compendium`, all `DRAFT`.
- 2026-11-23, founder: **the compendium ends with a recommendation**: where to start, what to check, what to leave. This settles the decision left open on 2026-11-22. The path, the home page ending and the synthetic sample compendium show it; the wording is `DRAFT`. The “Jedna decyzja” format keeps its own written recommendation.
- The guide form is a local demonstration like the contact form: it validates and shows a preview of the e-mail; it never sends, stores or generates anything. Blocks about tools are written and dated by the founder; never write their content as facts. The generated note appears only for two or more selected tools and is always labelled as generated. Open founder decisions (do not settle them): immediate or reviewed stack e-mail, update frequency and terms, e-mail provider, model for the notes, contact storage, consents, prices.
- 2026-11-10, founder: the consultation flow is the main example of work; the other examples (formats, example standard, analyses) are secondary.
- 2026-11-10, founder: state the consultation terms together (`site.flow.terms`): a meeting tailored to needs, any number of participants, and so on. Add more visual calls to action after the main sections. Develop the "Od czego zaczynają klienci" section further; the founder considers it a strong asset.
- 2026-11-10, founder: the consultation scenes use rich, scroll-driven animation; layout and quality were approved, the earlier version felt under-animated. Motion stays in the pencil-and-paper language and the page stays complete without it. Since 2026-11-23 these scenes are on the service page `/konsultacja/`, which is a real page again.
- 2026-11-23, founder: the home page is **the visitor's story**, and the site is more than the service (this replaces the 2026-11-22 order, in which the home page was the client path). The hero of the story is the visitor: an entrepreneur who feels they have to use AI because the competition does, facing a market too noisy to choose from. The consultation is the resolution of their problem; the story is not about Piotr Wrotny. Order of the home page: the visitor's situation (hero), the market's noise, the two reflexes (buy fast or wait), the turn (start from your own work), four steps, the ending (the recommendation), situations ("Która sytuacja jest Twoja?", without the format row), the person, the first-conversation card with the reading list under it. Content lives in `site.story`, all `DRAFT` except the headline. Do not add a section or a second "example of work" to the home page.
- 2026-11-23, founder: the home headline is **"Musisz coś zrobić z AI. Tylko co?"** (chosen 2026-11-23). "Konsultacje AI szyte na miarę." (approved 2026-11-10) is the headline of the service page. The **presentation of the service is its own page**, `/konsultacja/`: the offer, the client path (stages 0–5 with its closing screen and the consultation terms) and the three formats. "Czego nie robię" keeps its own page, `/czego-nie-robie/`; there is no separate analyses section.
- 2026-11-23, founder: the visitor is addressed directly ("Ty"); no entrepreneur, client or company is invented for the story. The papers of the market (offer, newsletter, invitation, team note) and the struck-out plan are illustrations: they quote no real offer, carry no numbers and stay labelled as examples. The pressure of the competition is told as the visitor's impression, never as a claim or a threat. The ending shows the recommendation of the synthetic sample compendium, labelled as synthetic.
- 2026-11-23, founder: the story is told **visually, as a small film scrubbed by the scroll**, with an anonymous little man in a suit as the visitor's stand-in: he sleeps, is woken up, the market's papers fly past him from left to right, "AI!" shouts from everywhere (the fridge included), he says "Muszę to uporządkować." and the consultation enters. The founder wants to play with this form; the story's core (being overwhelmed, the effort, the hero getting out) stays. The figure is nobody in particular: no name, no company, no likeness. The film is decoration over real text: without it (phone, reduced motion, no JavaScript) the page shows the flat sheets and is complete. The flat scenes keep their CSS 3D depth.
- 2026-11-23, founder: **the film is 2D, a pencil drawing on paper**, not 3D. The first cut (three.js, solids from code) looked crude and did not belong to the site's pencil-and-paper language; this replaces it. Built in `src/film.js` as one SVG drawn in code: shapes sketched by rough.js (cut-out paper with a pencil outline and hatching, each drawn twice so the line "boils"), moved by one GSAP timeline that the scroll scrubs; loaded on demand on wide screens with motion. No WebGL, no three.js. The scenario and the rules below are unchanged.
- 2026-11-23, founder: **the film has four acts and has to raise curiosity.** (1) The good world: sleep under a safe blanket. (2) The trouble: the hero meets difficulties, tension builds while he walks to the fridge and bursts with it; only then is the real problem recognised. (3) The solution: he leaves his details on this page (the card of the first conversation), takes the consultation and receives order; the phases of working together (contact, recognition, consultation, compendium) are elements of the story, each putting a part of the chaos in order. (4) The better world: after the consultation the hero is in a better place. Not back in bed: he sits calm and smiling at a tidy desk, no longer alone with his fear of AI but among a satisfied team who say things like "Wreszcie możemy działać." (lines in `site.story.ending.team`, `DRAFT`; figures of the story, never client quotes).
- 2026-11-23, founder: in the film **the consultation is the blue checking pencil** (the site's own pencil: blue confirms, red queries), a character that comes in, works with the hero and leaves when the work is done. It is not a person and not Piotr Wrotny; the story stays the visitor's. The people of the team are anonymous figures like the hero.
- 2026-11-23, with the four acts: while the film plays, the four steps stay one screen but are pinned, and each step's title arrives with its step in the film; the ending's flat compendium sheet is replaced by the same sheet as an object of the film (still the synthetic recommendation, still labelled). Without the film both screens are as before.
- 2026-11-23, founder, on the film: **the man really opens the fridge, and AI attacks him from inside.** The scene has to raise the question first (what happens when the man in the tie opens the fridge?): he walks over, the fridge gives itself away, he opens it, and only then comes the storm of AI. Nothing on the screen may give the answer away before the door opens.
- 2026-11-23, founder: **styles and formats must not overlap.** While the film plays, no flat sheet or table of the page lies on top of the animation; what the story needs to show at that moment (the card of the first conversation, for example) is an object of the film. Short captions beside the film are fine.
- 2026-11-23, founder: a screen must finish all of its own animation and then be held before it starts to leave. Do not let the exit begin while something on the screen is still being drawn.
- 2026-11-23, founder: `/konsultacja/` is to become a landing page built around the consultation sequence. To be discussed; do not redesign it before that.
- 2026-11-22, founder, on every page: **one message per screen, read top to bottom**; no content that does not move the visitor along the path, no zigzag between left and right. Before adding an element, name the visitor's question it answers; if another element already answers it, do not add it. Forms are interactive and show their result as the visitor acts (the guide form is the e-mail composing itself, the contact form is the card of the first conversation); the simplicity stays, the moment of interaction carries the effect.
- 2026-11-10, founder liked the visual direction, typefaces and quality of the `redesign/reimagined` branch (described in README). Extend it rather than replace it.
- Use-case placeholders are allowed: generic roles and situations that show the kind of question and the chosen format. They must be labelled as illustrative examples, never as engagements or clients. Content lives in `site.cases` and is marked `DRAFT` until approved.
- Voice content drafted by the assistant ("Czego nie robię", glossary, article theses, the hero illustration in `site.proof`) stays marked `DRAFT` until the founder approves the wording. One boundary statement per context; no factual caveat is removed.
- The example standard and the sample compendium are synthetic and must be labelled as such wherever they appear. The sample compendium uses an invented team unrelated to any engagement.
- The contact form is a local demonstration: validate and show a draft; never send data or claim a message has been sent.
- Content and navigation are Polish first, with separate editable content modules in `src/content`. Keep article pages and the synthetic example accessible without lead capture.
- The page must be fully readable without JavaScript and with `prefers-reduced-motion`.
